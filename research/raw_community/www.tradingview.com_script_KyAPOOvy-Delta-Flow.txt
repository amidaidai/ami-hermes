A lightweight volume-pressure dashboard designed to help traders identify shifts in buying and selling activity.
Features
- Real-time Delta calculation
- Delta Change momentum tracking
- Maximum and Minimum Delta readings
- Delta Percentage analysis
- Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD)
- Buyer vs Seller pressure gauge
- Compact dashboard designed for use alongside VWAP, Volume Profile and Opening Range Breakout (ORB) strategies
How It Works
Delta Flow estimates market pressure by comparing volume and candle direction. Positive readings suggest stronger buying activity, while negative readings suggest stronger selling activity.
The dashboard provides a quick overview of:
- Current buying/selling pressure
- Momentum shifts
- Recent extremes in market participation
- Longer-term cumulative pressure via CVD
- Best Used With
- VWAP
- Volume Profile
- ORB (Opening Range Breakout)
- Support and Resistance
- Trend-following strategies
- Important Note
This indicator uses TradingView volume data and candle structure to estimate buying and selling pressure. It does not use true bid/ask order flow or exchange-level footprint data.
