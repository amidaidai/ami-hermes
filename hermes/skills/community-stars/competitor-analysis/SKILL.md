# Competitor Analysis

Analyze competitors to identify strengths, weaknesses, and differentiation opportunities.

## Analysis Framework

### 1. Competitor Identification
Categorize competitors:
- **Direct**: Same problem, same solution approach
- **Indirect**: Same problem, different solution approach
- **Adjacent**: Related problem, could pivot
- **Potential**: Not in market yet but could enter

### 2. Competitive Dimensions
Analyze each competitor across:

| Dimension | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Product | Features, UX, quality, reliability |
| Pricing | Model, tiers, positioning |
| Distribution | Channels, sales model, partnerships |
| Marketing | Brand, content, community, SEO |
| Engineering | Tech stack, infra, talent |
| Support | Quality, hours, self-service |
| Finance | Funding, revenue, burn rate |

### 3. Competitive Positioning Map
Plot competitors on a 2x2 matrix (e.g., Price vs. Quality, Enterprise vs. Consumer):
- Identify whitespace opportunities (underserved areas)
- Identify red ocean clusters (overcrowded areas)

### 4. Strength/Weakness Analysis
For each competitor, list:
- **Top 3 strengths**: What they do better than anyone
- **Top 3 weaknesses**: Where they're vulnerable
- **Their strategy**: Are they commoditizing, differentiating, or retreating?
- **Our angle**: How we can exploit their weakness or avoid their strength

### 5. Feature Comparison Matrix

| Feature | Us | Comp A | Comp B | Comp C |
|---------|----|--------|--------|--------|
| Feature 1 | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ |
| Feature 2 | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Feature 3 | 🚧 | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |

### 6. Market Position Assessment
- **Market share trends**: Who's gaining/losing
- **Momentum**: Hiring, funding, press, product velocity
- **Customer sentiment**: Reviews, NPS, churn indicators
- **Partner ecosystem**: Who's aligning with whom

## Output Format

Competitor profiles (one per competitor) and a summary table with:
- Overall market map
- Key differentiators for us
- Risk areas to monitor
- Recommended competitive strategy
