# Startup Analysis

Analyze a startup from three distinct stakeholder perspectives.

## Analysis Perspectives

### VC / Investor Perspective
Focus on:
- **Market opportunity**: TAM/SAM/SOM, market growth rate
- **Traction**: Revenue growth, user growth, retention metrics
- **Unit economics**: CAC, LTV, payback period, gross margins
- **Team**: Founder-market fit, domain expertise, track record
- **Competitive moat**: Network effects, data advantages, switching costs
- **Capital efficiency**: Burn rate, runway, path to profitability
- **Valuation sanity**: Multiple, comparable companies
- **Exit potential**: Acquisition targets, IPO viability

### Job Applicant Perspective
Focus on:
- **Company health**: Funding stage, runway, growth trajectory
- **Culture**: Leadership style, WFH policy, diversity
- **Career growth**: Title progression, skill development
- **Compensation**: Salary, equity value, benefits
- **Risk**: Layoff history, pivot risk, market position
- **Role clarity**: Responsibilities, team size, reporting structure

### Founder Perspective
Focus on:
- **Problem-solution fit**: Is the problem real and painful?
- **Product-market fit**: Retention, NPS, organic growth signals
- **Go-to-market**: Channel strategy, CAC efficiency, sales cycle
- **Scale challenges**: Operational bottlenecks, hiring needs
- **Funding strategy**: When to raise, how much, from whom
- **Competitive landscape**: Winning vs. losing deals

## Output Format

Present three separate analyses (one per perspective). Each analysis includes:
- **Score**: 1-10 rating with rationale
- **Key strengths**
- **Key risks**
- **Recommended next steps**
- **Verdict**: Invest/Join/Found (or not) with conviction level
