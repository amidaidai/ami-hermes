# Strategy Red Team

Attack the load-bearing assumptions in a strategy to identify blind spots, failure modes, and unexamined risks before committing resources.

## Methodology

### Step 1: Identify Load-Bearing Assumptions
Extract every explicit and implicit assumption from the strategy. Load-bearing assumptions are those where:
- If the assumption is wrong, the strategy fails
- No evidence supports the assumption
- The assumption is convenient or optimistic

### Step 2: Apply Red Team Attacks
For each assumption, apply these adversarial lenses:

**The Competitor Lens**
- What if a competitor does exactly what we're planning, but faster/better/cheaper?
- What if they respond to our move in a way we haven't anticipated?
- What if a non-obvious competitor enters from an adjacent space?

**The Customer Lens**
- What if customers don't actually care about this problem?
- What if willingness-to-pay is 10x lower than assumed?
- What if switching costs are higher than anticipated?
- What if the early adopter segment doesn't represent the mainstream?

**The Execution Lens**
- What if the key hire doesn't work out?
- What if the timeline is 2x longer than planned?
- What if a critical dependency fails (regulatory, partnership, supply chain)?
- What if the cost structure is 50% higher than estimated?

**The Market Lens**
- What if the market is smaller than research suggests?
- What if growth rates slow or reverse?
- What if a substitute technology emerges?

### Step 3: Risk Scoring
For each identified risk, score:
- **Likelihood**: 1-5 (how likely is this failure mode?)
- **Impact**: 1-5 (how severe if it occurs?)
- **Detectability**: 1-5 (how quickly would we know?)

### Step 4: Recommend Mitigations
For each high-risk assumption, recommend:
- **Test**: How to validate the assumption cheaply
- **Monitor**: Leading indicators to watch
- **Contingency**: What to do if it fails
- **Adapt**: How to build optionality into the strategy
