# Last 30 Days Social Media Research

Research social media sentiment and discussions across Reddit, X (Twitter), YouTube, and Hacker News about a topic, product, company, or trend over the last 30 days.

## Research Process

### 1. Define the Query
Identify the exact topic, keywords, and filter criteria:
- Primary keyword(s) and variations
- Exclude terms (e.g., if searching for "apple", exclude "fruit")
- Date range: last 30 days

### 2. Search Each Platform

**Reddit**
- Search r/all for mentions, posts, comments
- Look at upvote count as engagement signal
- Check relevant subreddits specifically
- Note: AskReddit, popular posts, and comments

**X (Twitter/X)**
- Search for recent posts with the keyword
- Look at engagement (likes, retweets, replies)
- Note influential accounts posting about the topic
- Check trending topics if applicable

**YouTube**
- Search for recent videos (sort by date)
- Note view counts, like/dislike ratios
- Check comment sections on popular videos

**Hacker News**
- Search news.ycombinator.com for stories
- Look at points, comment count, and discussion quality
- Pay attention to top-level comments

### 3. Sentiment Analysis
Classify each mention:
- 🟢 **Positive**: Praise, recommendation, enthusiasm
- 🟡 **Neutral**: Informational, balanced, no strong opinion
- 🔴 **Negative**: Criticism, complaints, warnings
- 💬 **Question**: Asking for help/information

### 4. Aggregate Findings

Produce a summary with:
- **Overall sentiment**: Positive/Neutral/Negative breakdown
- **Key themes**: What people are talking about
- **Notable voices**: Influential people/accounts
- **Emerging trends**: What's gaining attention
- **Critical issues**: Main complaints or concerns
- **Engagement level**: How much discussion exists

## Output Format

A markdown report organized by platform, with an executive summary at the top. Include representative samples (quotes with context) and any notable links.
