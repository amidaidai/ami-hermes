# Competitive Battlecard Generator

Create sales-ready competitive battlecards that equip sales teams to win deals against specific competitors.

## Battlecard Structure

### 1. Competitor Overview
- Company name, size, funding, key products
- Primary market focus and customer profile
- Key strengths and weaknesses

### 2. Competitive Positioning
- How we win: The top 3 reasons prospects choose us over them
- How they win: Why prospects sometimes pick them (be honest)
- Head-to-head comparison table

### 3. Objection Handling
For each common objection, provide:
- **Objection**: Exact phrasing a prospect might use
- **Acknowledge**: Empathetic acknowledgment
- **Reframe**: Shift perspective
- **Proof point**: Evidence, case study, or data point
- **Bridge**: Transition to our strengths

### 4. Differentiators
The 3-5 most impactful differences that change buying decisions. Include:
- Feature/function comparison
- Pricing difference
- Customer experience difference
- Ecosystem/integration difference

### 5. Trigger Events
Signals that a prospect may be open to switching:
- Contract renewal approaching
- Pricing increase from competitor
- Product roadmap gaps
- Support quality decline
- M&A activity

### 6. Proof Points
- Customer testimonials
- Analyst recognition
- Case studies
- Performance benchmarks

## Output Format

Present as a one-page reference card (markdown) that a sales rep can review in 2 minutes before a call. Include a "cheat sheet" section with the top 3 key messages.
