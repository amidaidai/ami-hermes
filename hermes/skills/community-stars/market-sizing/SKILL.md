# Market Sizing

Calculate Total Addressable Market (TAM), Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM), and Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) using both top-down and bottom-up approaches.

## Key Concepts

### TAM (Total Addressable Market)
The total revenue opportunity available if a product or service achieves 100% market share.

### SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)
The segment of the TAM your product can realistically reach given your distribution channels, geography, and business model.

### SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)
The portion of SAM you can realistically capture in the short-to-medium term (typically 3-5 years).

## Approaches

### Top-Down Approach
Start with a large macro figure and apply narrowing assumptions:
1. Find reliable industry report data (Gartner, IDC, Statista, government sources)
2. Apply percentage filters for relevant segments
3. State every assumption explicitly and cite sources

### Bottom-Up Approach
Start with unit economics and scale up:
1. Define unit of sale (subscription, transaction, product)
2. Determine price per unit
3. Estimate realistic customer count based on segmentation
4. Multiply: Customers × Price × Penetration Rate

### Value Theory Approach
Estimate willingness-to-pay based on value delivered to customers:
1. Quantify the customer's current cost or pain
2. Estimate the value your solution provides
3. Price as a fraction of value captured

## Output Format

Present results in a markdown table:

| Metric | Top-Down | Bottom-Up | Final Estimate |
|--------|----------|-----------|----------------|
| TAM    | $X       | $Y        | $Z             |
| SAM    | $X       | $Y        | $Z             |
| SOM    | $X       | $Y        | $Z             |

Include all assumptions, sources, and confidence ratings for each estimate.
