# SWOT Analysis

Perform a comprehensive SWOT analysis and derive actionable strategic recommendations.

## The Framework

### Strengths (Internal, Positive)
What does the organization do well? Consider:
- Resources and capabilities
- Intellectual property
- Brand and reputation
- Cost advantages
- Network effects
- Talent and culture

### Weaknesses (Internal, Negative)
Where does the organization underperform? Consider:
- Resource gaps
- Process inefficiencies
- Brand perception issues
- Technology debt
- Talent gaps
- Market concentration risk

### Opportunities (External, Positive)
What external factors can be leveraged? Consider:
- Market trends
- Regulatory changes
- Technology shifts
- Competitor weaknesses
- New customer segments
- Partnership possibilities

### Threats (External, Negative)
What external factors could harm the business? Consider:
- New entrants
- Substitute products
- Regulatory changes
- Economic downturns
- Supply chain risks
- Technology disruption

## Deriving Recommendations

Use the TOWS Matrix to create four categories of actions:

| | Strengths (S) | Weaknesses (W) |
|--|--------------|----------------|
| **Opportunities (O)** | SO Strategies: Use strengths to pursue opportunities | WO Strategies: Improve weaknesses to pursue opportunities |
| **Threats (T)** | ST Strategies: Use strengths to defend against threats | WT Strategies: Minimize weaknesses to avoid threats |

## Output Format

Present as:
1. **SWOT Quadrant**: Each quadrant with 5-8 bullet points
2. **TOWS Matrix**: 4-6 actionable strategies derived from cross-analysis
3. **Priority Recommendations**: Top 3 actions ranked by impact/effort
4. **Key Metrics**: How to measure progress on each recommendation
