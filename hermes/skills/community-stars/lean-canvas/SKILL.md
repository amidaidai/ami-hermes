# Lean Canvas Generator

Generate a complete Lean Canvas based on a product or business idea.

## The Lean Canvas Structure

A Lean Canvas consists of 9 blocks, filled in this order:

### 1. Problem
List the top 1-3 problems your target customers face. Frame problems as jobs-to-be-done or pains.

**Existing alternatives:** How are customers solving these problems today?

### 2. Customer Segments
List your target customers and early adopters. Be specific — don't say "everyone".

### 3. Unique Value Proposition (UVP)
A single, clear, compelling message that turns an unaware visitor into an interested prospect. Must be:
- Specific to your solution
- Focused on the outcome, not the features
- Different from competitors

### 4. Solution
Outline the minimal possible solution for each problem identified above.

### 5. Channels
How will you reach your customer segments? Include both inbound (content, SEO, social) and outbound (sales, partnerships, ads) paths.

### 6. Revenue Streams
How does the business make money? Include pricing model, revenue per customer, and lifetime value.

### 7. Cost Structure
List fixed and variable costs to operate the business: hosting, salaries, marketing, COGS.

### 8. Key Metrics
Define the key activities you measure to know the business is on track (e.g., DAU, MRR, CAC, churn rate).

### 9. Unfair Advantage
Something that cannot be easily copied or bought. Examples: network effects, brand, proprietary data, community.

## Output Format

Present the canvas as a structured markdown document with each block clearly labeled. Include the rationale behind each block.

For each block, add a confidence rating: 🟢 High / 🟡 Medium / 🔴 Low — to identify risky assumptions that need validation first.
