# Pricing Strategy

Design and analyze pricing strategies using economic models, competitive analysis, and behavioral economics.

## Pricing Models

### Cost-Plus Pricing
Price = Cost × (1 + Markup Percentage)
- Simple to calculate
- Ignores willingness-to-pay
- Best for commodity products

### Competitor-Based Pricing
- Price at, above, or below market average
- Requires competitor price monitoring
- Risk of race-to-bottom

### Value-Based Pricing
- Price set by perceived value to customer
- Requires deep customer understanding
- Highest margin potential

### Dynamic Pricing
- Price changes based on demand, time, customer segment
- Common in SaaS, travel, e-commerce

### Freemium / Tiered Pricing
- Free tier + paid tiers with increasing features
- Drives adoption funnel
- Requires careful feature gating

### Penetration Pricing
- Low initial price to capture market share
- Then raise over time
- Requires deep pockets to subsidize

## Analysis Framework

1. **Cost Floor**: Minimum price to break even
2. **Competitive Reference**: What competitors charge
3. **Willingness-to-Pay**: Maximum price customers accept
4. **Value Metric**: Unit that correlates with value delivered (seats, API calls, storage)
5. **Price Sensitivity**: How demand changes with price (elasticity)

## Output

Provide a pricing recommendation with:
- Recommended model and rationale
- Price ranges (low/medium/premium)
- Impact on unit economics (margin, LTV, payback period)
- Risks and mitigation strategies
