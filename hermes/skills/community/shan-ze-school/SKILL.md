# 杉泽 School — New-Oriental Mythic Ink-and-Color Fantasy

Generate image prompts in the style of 杉泽 (Shan Ze) school — a contemporary new-oriental ink-and-color fantasy art style that blends traditional Chinese ink wash painting with fantastical mythological subjects.

## About the Style

杉泽 style is characterized by:

- **Ink wash (水墨)** — flowing, organic ink strokes with varying opacity
- **Vibrant color accents** — particularly reds, golds, teals against monochrome ink
- **Mythological subjects** — Chinese dragons, qilin, phoenixes, mountain spirits, celestial beings
- **Ethereal atmosphere** — mist, clouds, flowing robes, floating elements
- **Delicate linework** — fine, energetic brush lines defining forms
- **Layered transparency** — overlapping washes of color and ink
- **Fantasy elements** — glowing effects, luminous eyes, magical auras

## Composition Tips

1. **Negative space** — leave large areas of blank paper for the ink to "breathe"
2. **Diagonal flow** — subjects often ascend diagonally, conveying motion
3. **Mist transitions** — use mist to fade edges into the paper
4. **Scale contrast** — tiny figures against vast landscapes
5. **Calligraphic elements** — include Chinese characters or seal stamps as compositional anchors
6. **Layering** — ink wash background, then detailed foreground subjects

## Prompt Negative (to avoid)

Avoid these elements that clash with the style:

- Photorealism / photographic details
- Western fantasy tropes (wizards, castles, European dragons)
- Hard digital edges / sharp vector art
- Neon colors (except for specific magical accents)
- Modern/anachronistic elements

## Pitfalls

- AI models may default to generic "Chinese painting" instead of the specific 杉泽 aesthetic — be very specific about ink wash, color accents, and line quality
- Include "xuan paper texture" to get the characteristic paper grain
- The style works best at vertical or square aspect ratios (3:4 or 1:1)
- Too many colors will overwhelm the ink-wash foundation — limit to 2-3 accent colors per piece
- Avoid over-specifying — leave room for the AI to interpret the brushwork
