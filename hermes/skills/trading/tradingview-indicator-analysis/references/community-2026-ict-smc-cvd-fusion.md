## Core Community Consensus
- **Liquidity Sweep is the soul**: Institutions hunt stops at equal highs/lows, BSL/SSL, prior session highs/lows. "The entire methodology is built on this one idea: institutions need your stops to fill their orders."
- **CVD/Order Flow anchoring**: CVD only matters when anchored to structure (sweep + key level). Divergence (price LL + CVD HL or vice versa) + absorption at sweep = high-probability reversal. Isolated CVD = noise.
- **Displacement confirmation**: Strong body, tiny wicks, aggressive one-directional move after sweep = institutional intent. Must pair with sweep + CVD.
- **Kill Zones as filter (esp. XAU)**: London (2-5AM EST) highest prob for sweeps/Judas Swing; NY AM for continuation. Asia builds range/liquidity. Non-KZ sessions: lower priority or silent.
- **Volume Profile integration**: VAH/VAL/POC + Naked POC/HVN-LVN as magnets or rejection zones. Combine with ICT levels (OB/FVG).
- **Gold/XAU specific sequence**: Asia range build → London sweep → NY/CVD + Displacement confirmation. HTF bullish bias often due to institutional flows.
- **BTC specifics**: Spot vs Perp CVD differentiation to distinguish real demand vs leverage-driven moves.
- **Real backtest reality**: Claimed 70-80% win rates usually backtest to 50-65%. Require R:R ≥2, ≥20 real review samples before weight adjustments. Structure on 4H first is non-negotiable.
