```yaml
  theme: "corporate"

      icon: "rocket"
    
    - header: "2020"

      icon: "chart-up"
```

## Design Rules

1. **Visual hierarchy** — Most important info largest/most prominent
2. **Flow direction** — Left-to-right or top-to-bottom
3. **White space** — 15-20% empty space minimum
4. **Color limit** — 3-5 colors max (including background)
5. **Font limit** — 2 font families max
6. **Data-ink ratio** — Remove non-data ink (gridlines, borders when unnecessary)

## Pitfalls

- Don't overload — an infographic should convey 3-5 key messages max
- Avoid 3D charts — they distort perception of values
- Ensure proper attribution for data sources
- Check accessibility: color-blind friendly palettes, readable font sizes
- Test mobile readability — infographics are often viewed on phones
