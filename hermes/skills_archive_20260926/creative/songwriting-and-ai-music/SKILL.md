# Songwriting & AI Music Prompts

A skill for crafting song lyrics, structuring compositions, and generating effective prompts for Suno AI and other AI music platforms.

## Songwriting Fundamentals

### Lyric Structure
- **Verses** — Tell the story, set the scene (4-8 lines each)
- **Chorus** — The hook, repeated, emotionally resonant (4-6 lines)
- **Bridge** — Perspective shift or emotional climax (4-8 lines)
- **Pre-chorus** — Builds tension before the chorus drop (2-4 lines)
- **Outro** — Resolution, fade-out

### Rhyme Schemes
- AABB — Couplet rhyme (simple, catchy)
- ABAB — Alternating rhyme (classic, smooth)
- ABCB — Ballad rhyme (folk, storytelling)
- Free verse — No rhyme (spoken word, modern)

### Emotional Mapping
Match musical elements to emotional intent:
| Emotion | Key | Tempo | Dynamics |
|---------|-----|-------|----------|
| Happy | Major (C, G, D) | 120-140 BPM | Bright, staccato |
| Sad | Minor (Am, Em) | 60-80 BPM | Legato, sparse |
| Angry | Minor (Dm, Fm) | 140-180 BPM | Heavy, distorted |
| Hopeful | Major (F, C) | 80-100 BPM | Building, anthemic |

## Suno AI Prompt Engineering

### Prompt Structure
```
[Genre tags] [Mood/atmosphere] [Instrumentation] [Vocal style]
[Production quality] [Tempo/bpm] [Reference artists]

LYRICS:
[Verse 1]
...
[Chorus]
...
```

### Genre Tags (examples)
- `pop`, `rock`, `indie folk`, `synthwave`, `lo-fi hip hop`
- `acoustic ballad`, `house`, `dnb`, `ambient electronic`
- `cinematic orchestral`, `jazz`, `blues`, `country`

### Mood Descriptors
`upbeat`, `melancholic`, `dreamy`, `dark`, `ethereal`, `driving`, `mellow`, `aggressive`, `nostalgic`, `triumphant`

### Instrumentation Tags
`acoustic guitar`, `piano`, `synth pads`, `808 drums`, `strings`, `brass`, `electric guitar`, `analog synth`

### Vocal Style
`male vocals`, `female vocals`, `duet`, `choir`, `raspy`, `falsetto`, `spoken word`, `harmony`, `rap verse`

## Workflow

1. **Define intent** — mood, message, audience
2. **Structure the song** — verse/chorus/bridge layout
3. **Write lyrics** — follow chosen structure and rhyme scheme
4. **Create Suno prompt** — genre + mood + instrumentation + vocal style
5. **Generate and iterate** — refine prompt based on output
6. **Polish** — adjust lyrics, try different genre tags

## Pitfalls

- Too many genre tags can confuse the AI (stick to 2-3)
- Suno ignores extremely long lyrics; keep verses tight
- Rhyme-forced lyrics sound amateurish — prioritize natural phrasing
- Generic prompts produce generic results — be specific about vibe
