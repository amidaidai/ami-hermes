# Government PPT Writing Style Reference

Detailed reference for writing government/official training PPT content in correct administrative register.

## Core Rule

Government training PPTs target 镇村干部 (township/village cadres). The language must be flat, descriptive, and administrative — the same register used in official notices and work reports. No consulting frameworks, no catchy names, no rhetorical constructions.

## Title Style: Banned vs Correct

| Banned (AI/consulting style) | Correct (government style) |
|------------------------------|---------------------------|
| 底数要清 | 耕地保护基本情况 |
| 政策要准 | 政策法规变化与面临形势 |
| 任务要实 | 年度重点工作安排 |
| 闭环要硬 | 存在的主要困难及建议 |
| 守线结果：有余量，但不能把余量当作可占空间 | 2025年度耕地保护目标完成情况 |
| 耕地保护空间：划足、留余、把恢复潜力落到图上 | 耕地保护空间划定情况 |
| 永久基本农田正向优化：优进劣出，不是随意调线 | 永久基本农田正向优化方案 |
| 遥感监测压力：问题线索明显增加，核查速度决定工作主动 | 国土变更调查遥感监测情况 |
| 六个变化：镇村工作的判断标准已经变了 | 当前耕地保护政策主要变化 |
| 形势判断：博白正处在"五重压力叠加期" | 当前面临的主要形势 |
| 政策边界：镇村最容易混淆的六件事 | 基层工作中需注意的政策界限 |
| 年度作战图：三项攻坚、两个支撑、一个闭环 | 年度重点工作总体安排 |

| 培训后，先把四件事做起来 | 下一步工作要求 |

## Body Text: Banned Patterns

### "不是…而是…" construction
- ❌ "不是新增建设指标，不是随意调整红线的空间"
- ✅ "该余量为年度考核结果，不作为建设用地审批依据"

### Exclamation / imperative
- ❌ "先做真恢复、能耕种、管得住的3000亩！"
- ✅ "本年度恢复任务应优先选择具备稳定耕作条件的地块"

### Coined framework names
- ❌ "五重压力叠加期" "作战图" "安全垫" "一图两表一台账" "红黄绿管理" "三优先" "周循环" "系统战" "硬约束"
- ✅ "当前面临的主要形势" "年度重点工作安排" "考核余量" "工作台账" "进度管理" "工作优先序" "工作推进机制"

### Anti-pattern callouts ("不能…""严禁…")
- ❌ "不能只清表、旋耕、临时撒种"
- ✅ "应核实灌排条件、土壤状况和后续管护安排"

### Colloquial section subtitles
- ❌ "先把考核任务、规划划定、年度监测三种口径分开，再看安全余量和风险所在"
- ✅ "耕地保护目标完成情况、空间划定及遥感监测"

## Deadline Urgency Expression

When a task has a near-term deadline (e.g., "6月底前完成30%"), do NOT bury the urgency in body text. Use explicit callout boxes with temporal framing:

- ❌ "6月底目标：完成30%整治任务" (plain, no urgency)
- ✅ "6月底目标（攻坚期）：完成30%整治任务\n应达733.70亩，差距673.29亩\n距6月底仅剩约两周，须逐日倒排" (urgency explicit)

The word "攻坚期" (critical push period) is standard administrative language for deadline urgency. "逐日倒排" (countdown by day) is also standard. Do NOT use "冲刺""决战" or other military metaphors.

## Content Completeness Checklist for Training Decks

Government training PPTs for county/township cadres must include ALL of the following. Missing any of these makes the deck look incomplete:

1. **Training Summary / Q&A page (培训小结)** — A dedicated summary slide before the closing actions. Lists the 3-5 core takeaways, confirms key data points, and gives the主持人 a natural transition to现场交流. This is expected at every face-to-face training session.
2. **Organization structure page** — When the training unit is an office (办公室) responsible for implementing a system (e.g., 田长制), include a dedicated slide showing the organizational hierarchy, each level's responsible personnel, and their specific duties. Do not assume the audience already knows who does what.
3. **Latest policy interpretation page** — If a new policy was issued within the current calendar year (e.g., 自然资发〔2026〕38号 in March 2026), include a dedicated slide explaining its key points and local implications. Do not rely on a timeline alone — timelines list titles but do not interpret content.
4. **Department responsibility division** — A table or list showing which department handles what, with legal basis (article numbers).
5. **Policy abolition/replacement relationships** — Explicitly show old policies that are abolished and what replaced them, with effective dates.
6. **Superior planning basis** — Hierarchical task decomposition (province → city → county) with each level's plan full name and document number.
7. **"Eight Prohibitions" or equivalent regulatory requirements** — Spell out specific prohibitions, not just reference the document.
8. **Local case studies** — Concrete examples with village/town names and specific numbers.
9. **Legislation in progress** — Note draft laws and their审议 status.

## Context-Specific Notes

- For Guangxi/博白县 context: the 自治区 level documents and 田长制 framework are important reference points
- 镇村干部 audience: avoid jargon, use concrete operational language
- When presenting data: state the number and its source directly, do not frame with analysis labels
- Speaker notes: brief, professional, indicate what to emphasize — not coaching language
